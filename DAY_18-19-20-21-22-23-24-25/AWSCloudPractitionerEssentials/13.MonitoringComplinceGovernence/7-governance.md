# Governance in the AWS Cloud  

In this lesson, you will review the following three AWS services that help **govern and enforce services and accounts** to meet your company's requirements:  

---

## AWS Control Tower  
- Provides a **preconfigured landing zone** for multi-account AWS environments.  
- Automates setup of **best-practice governance controls** such as guardrails.  
- Integrates with AWS Organizations to help enforce account baselines.  
- Simplifies **account provisioning** with security, compliance, and logging pre-enabled.  
- Benefits: Quickly establish a secure, compliant AWS environment at scale.  

---

## AWS Service Catalog  
- Enables you to create, manage, and distribute **approved portfolios of resources**.  
- Ensures teams can only launch **pre-approved services** that comply with organizational standards.  
- Helps enforce **configuration consistency** across accounts and regions.  
- Benefits: Reduces risk, enforces standardization, and speeds up deployment with pre-vetted templates.  

---

## AWS License Manager  
- Helps you track, manage, and enforce **software licenses** across AWS and on-premises environments.  
- Prevents over-deployment of licensed software by setting **usage limits**.  
- Provides centralized visibility into license usage for compliance and cost control.  
- Supports licensing for vendors such as Microsoft, Oracle, and SAP.  
- Benefits: Simplifies license management, prevents compliance violations, and optimizes costs.
