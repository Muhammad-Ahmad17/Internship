
### **Health of Your AWS Cloud Resources**

**Notifications on Service Events**

* **AWS Health** is your primary source for information on events and changes affecting your AWS resources.
* It provides notifications about:

  * **Service events** (outages, disruptions)
  * **Planned changes** (maintenance or upgrades)
  * **Account-specific notifications**

---

### **AWS Health Dashboard**

* Lets you **view account-specific health information** and get **event updates**.

* Can be used **programmatically** via the **AWS Health API** (requires AWS Premium Support).

* **Benefits:**

  * Timely, actionable guidance to **remedy issues**.
  * Helps **manage overall service health**.
  * Integrated for **automation and scalability**.

* **Use Cases:**

  * Monitor account-specific resource health.
  * Plan for lifecycle events.
  * Troubleshoot incidents efficiently.
