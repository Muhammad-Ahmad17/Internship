
## 🌐 AWS Well-Architected Framework – Six Pillars

1. **Operational Excellence** → Operations, monitoring, automation, and continuous improvement.
2. **Security** → Protect systems and data (least privilege, integrity, detection).
3. **Reliability** → Recovery planning, fault tolerance, adaptability.
4. **Performance Efficiency** → Use the right resources, scale as needs change.
5. **Cost Optimization** → Control and reduce costs via smart provisioning.
6. **Sustainability** → Design for energy efficiency and environmental responsibility.

---

## 🛠 AWS Well-Architected Tool (WA Tool)

* **Free service** to review workloads against the 6 pillars.
* Provides:

  * Workload reviews & improvement plans.
  * Milestone tracking.
  * Custom lenses (tailored evaluations).
* **Integrations** → IAM, APIs, dashboards for collaboration.
* **Goal** → Ensure workloads are **secure, reliable, efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable**.

---

👉 In short:

* **Framework = the 6 pillars (best practices)**.
* **WA Tool = the hands-on service to review and apply those practices**.
