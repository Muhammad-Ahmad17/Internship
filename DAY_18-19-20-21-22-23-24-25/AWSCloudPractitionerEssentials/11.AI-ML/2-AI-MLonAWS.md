# AWS AI and ML Service Tiers

## Tier 1: Pre-built AWS AI Services

Pre-built, ready-to-use AI models for specific business use cases.

### Language Services

* **Amazon Comprehend** – Extracts insights from text (content classification, sentiment analysis).
* **Amazon Polly** – Converts text into lifelike speech (virtual assistants, accessibility).
* **Amazon Transcribe** – Converts speech to text (call transcription, subtitling).
* **Amazon Translate** – Real-time text translation (document translation, multilingual apps).

### Computer Vision and Search Services

* **Amazon Kendra** – Context-aware enterprise search (chatbots, application search).
* **Amazon Rekognition** – Image/video analysis (content moderation, identity verification).
* **Amazon Textract** – Extracts text from documents/forms (finance, healthcare, government).

### Conversational AI and Personalization Services

* **Amazon Lex** – Builds conversational interfaces (chatbots, voice assistants).
* **Amazon Personalize** – Provides personalized recommendations (streaming, e-commerce).

## Tier 2: ML Services

Customizable ML solutions without managing infrastructure.

### Amazon SageMaker AI

* Build, train, and deploy ML models.
* Pre-trained models available.
* Integrated development environment for managing workflows.

**Key Benefits of SageMaker AI:**

* Choice of ML tools
* Fully managed infrastructure
* Repeatable ML workflows

---

## Tier 3: ML Frameworks and Infrastructure

For organizations needing complete control over ML training and deployment.

### ML Frameworks

* Supported frameworks: **PyTorch, Apache MXNet, TensorFlow**.

### AWS ML Infrastructure

* ML-optimized **Amazon EC2 instances**
* **Amazon EMR** (big data processing)
* **Amazon ECS** (containerized ML workloads)
